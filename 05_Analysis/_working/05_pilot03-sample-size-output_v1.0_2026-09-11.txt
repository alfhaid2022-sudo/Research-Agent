6. KAPPA - precision, and the prevalence paradox
==============================================================================
Design: per antigen, one serologic call and one genotype-PREDICTED call
per donor -> a 2x2 table per antigen. Kappa is computed PER ANTIGEN.
Kappa is NOT computed by pooling antigen-donor pairs across antigens:
pooling mixes tables with different marginals and correlated errors
within donor, and the pooled kappa is then uninterpretable (D025's
antigen-donor pair unit governs the 7-class taxonomy tabulation and the
overall percent agreement, which is why that percent agreement needs a
donor-clustered variance - see section 7).

  the lead statistic. The lead statistics for those antigens are the full

  each with exact 95% CIs. PABAK is reported alongside kappa for ALL
  antigens - and is explicitly labelled as a monotone relabelling of
  observed agreement (2*po-1) that assumes balanced marginals the study
  does not have. PABAK is context, never evidence of good agreement.
  The 10%-90% rule is mechanical and declared before any data are seen,
  so it cannot be used to select a favourable statistic after the fact.

7. MULTI-CENTRE CLUSTERING - design effect
==============================================================================
Donors cluster by centre (the sampling unit) and are cross-classified
by ancestry. Treating donors as independent would falsely narrow every
CI in this study. ICC is UNKNOWN and is bracketed, not assumed.

DEFF = 1 + ((cv^2+1)*mbar - 1)*ICC   (Kish, unequal cluster sizes)

  ICC ESTIMATION (pre-specified, SAP sec.9): for each allele/antigen,
  fit a random-intercept logistic model with centre as the random effect
  and report BOTH the latent-scale ICC (sigma_u^2 / (sigma_u^2 + pi^2/3))
  and the proportion-scale ICC (ANOVA/method-of-moments). The two scales
  are NOT interchangeable and are commonly confused; both are reported
  with the scale named. With few centres the between-centre variance is
  estimated on very few degrees of freedom, so the ICC itself will carry
  a wide interval - which is reported, not suppressed.

  ANALYSIS CONSEQUENCE: with ~6-10 centres the GEE sandwich variance is
  anti-conservative. The SAP pre-specifies the Mancl-DeRouen bias-reduced
  covariance with a t reference distribution on (K-1) df, and a
  cluster bootstrap resampling CENTRES as the sensitivity analysis.

==============================================================================
7b. THE BINDING CONSTRAINT IS THE NUMBER OF CENTRES, NOT THE NUMBER OF DONORS
==============================================================================
Once between-centre heterogeneity is real, Var(pooled p) tends to
tau^2/K as donors per centre grows, where tau^2 is the between-centre
variance of the centre-specific proportions and K the number of centres.
No number of donors can beat that limit. tau relates to the ICC by
tau^2 = ICC * p(1-p).

Illustrated for RHCE*ce(733G), p = 67/708 = 9.46% (GULF Madkhali2025):

  0.005                  2.070pp  +/-1.66pp  +/-1.43pp  +/-1.17pp  +/-0.91pp
  0.010                  2.927pp  +/-2.34pp  +/-2.03pp  +/-1.66pp  +/-1.28pp

  This is the floor on the achievable 95% CI half-width, attained only
  in the limit of infinitely many donors per centre. Adding a 13th centre
  does more for precision than doubling every centre's intake.
  It also uses a normal approximation on K centre-level proportions; with
  K < 15 the SAP substitutes a t(K-1) reference distribution, which widens
  the interval further (t(5)=2.571 vs z=1.960, i.e. ~31% wider at K=6).

  independent observations - which is a common and serious error.

-- PRE-SPECIFIED PRIMARY PRECISION CRITERION --
  (a) The frequency of RHCE*ce(733G), the commonest clinically relevant
      RHCE variant and the only one with more than a handful of Gulf
      observations, estimated with a 95% CI half-width <= 2.0 percentage
      points AFTER the design effect.
  (b) Kappa for each of D, C, E, c estimated with a 95% CI half-width
      <= 0.10. Antigen e is excluded from criterion (b) by the >90%
      prevalence rule in section 6 and is governed by PPA/NPA instead.
  Criterion (a) is the binding one.

  Designs meeting criterion (a) at the mid ICC bracket (0.010):
    6 centres x 500 donors = 3,000 (DEFF 6.44, n_eff 466, +/-1.88pp)
    8 centres x 250 donors = 2,000 (DEFF 3.71, n_eff 538, +/-1.75pp)
    8 centres x 375 donors = 3,000 (DEFF 5.08, n_eff 591, +/-1.67pp)
    12 centres x 250 donors = 3,000 (DEFF 3.71, n_eff 808, +/-1.43pp)
    12 centres x 333 donors = 3,996 (DEFF 4.62, n_eff 865, +/-1.38pp)
    12 centres x 500 donors = 6,000 (DEFF 6.44, n_eff 932, +/-1.33pp)
    20 centres x 250 donors = 5,000 (DEFF 3.71, n_eff 1346, +/-1.11pp)

  Criterion (b) requires an EFFECTIVE n of 492 donors (worst of D/C/E/c at kappa=0.80).
  The recommended design below delivers n_eff of 808 at ICC=0.010 and 467 at ICC=0.020,
  i.e. criterion (b) is met up to ICC ~0.020 and is MARGINAL beyond it.
  Kappa is not the binding constraint; the allele frequency is.

-- RECOMMENDED DESIGN --
  n = 3,000 donors, allocated as 12 centres x 250 donors, with a hard
  floor of 8 centres and at least one centre in each of the six GCC
  states that can participate. Rationale: 12 x 250 and 6 x 500 have the
  same total n, but at ICC=0.010 they give n_eff of 808 and 466
  respectively. Spreading the same 3,000 donors over twice as many
  centres nearly doubles the information content at identical cost.
  Achieved precision on RHCE*ce(733G) across the ICC bracket:
    ICC=0.002  DEFF= 1.54  n_eff=  1944  +/-0.92pp
    ICC=0.005  DEFF= 2.36  n_eff=  1273  +/-1.14pp
    ICC=0.010  DEFF= 3.71  n_eff=   808  +/-1.43pp
    ICC=0.020  DEFF= 6.43  n_eff=   467  +/-1.88pp
    ICC=0.050  DEFF=14.58  n_eff=   206  +/-2.84pp

  HONEST STATEMENT REQUIRED IN THE PROTOCOL: if the ICC turns out to be
  at the top of this bracket (0.05), n = 3,000 delivers a half-width of
  +/-2.84pp on a 9.5% allele (n_eff falls to 206) - wide, and the
  kappa criterion then fails as well. This is not rescuable by recruiting
  more donors. It is rescuable only by recruiting more centres. The
  protocol must therefore treat centre recruitment, not donor
  recruitment, as the primary feasibility risk.

==============================================================================
8b. NOT SUPPORTED AT ANY FEASIBLE n - stated as a design finding
==============================================================================
  1. RHD*r's-RHCE*ce(733G,1006T) (0.28% allele frequency, based on TWO
     observed alleles) to useful relative precision. At n=3,000 donors
     with DEFF 3.7 the effective n is ~810 donors = ~1,620 alleles;
     expected count 4.6 alleles. Below the pre-declared E>=5 floor.
  2. RHCE*ce(712G) (0.42%, three observed alleles): expected count
     6.9 alleles at the same effective n. Also below the floor.
  3. RHD*DVI at the US bracket of 1 in 731 (0.137%): even 10,000
     INDEPENDENT donors give a CI whose upper limit is >3x its lower.
  4. DEL frequency among serologic D-negative donors, if the D-negative
     stratum is sampled at its natural prevalence. At the Thai bracket
     (7.60%) and +/-2pp this needs ~681 D-negative donors; at an
     unsourced pi_Dneg of 5-10% that is 7,000-14,000 total donors before
     any design effect. WITH a DEFF of ~3.7 it is 25,000-50,000.

  CONSEQUENCE, PRE-SPECIFIED: these four are NOT frequency-estimation
  outcomes. They are reported as CASE ASCERTAINMENT - 'k carriers
  identified among n donors tested (exact 95% CI a% to b%)' - with the
  explicit label 'not estimable to useful precision at this sample size'.
  No frequency claim, no between-group comparison, no extrapolation to
  the GCC donor pool is permitted for them.

  DESIGN REMEDY the protocol should consider for DEL specifically:
  ENRICHED SAMPLING of the serologic D-negative stratum (test all D-
  negative donors presenting over a longer window, rather than a fixed
  fraction), optionally further enriched to C-positive D-negative donors.
  This changes the DEL estimand from a donor-pool frequency to a
  conditional frequency P(DEL | serologic D-negative), which is the
  clinically actionable quantity anyway, and it is achievable. The
  enrichment MUST be declared in the protocol, because a conditional
  frequency presented as a pool frequency would be a misrepresentation.

  * pi_Dneg = prevalence of serologic D-negativity in the GCC donor pool
    The scoping file contains NO GCC donor-pool D-negative prevalence. Madkhali's RHD arm (n=60) and Alalshaikh's 70 D+/66 D- are SELECTED, not prevalence, samples.
