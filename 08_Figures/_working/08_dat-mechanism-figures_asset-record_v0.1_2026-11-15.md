## Conventions (stated in the legends and speaker notes)
- **Solid lines only.** Every depicted mechanism is stated in the source. No hypothesised steps are drawn, so no dashed lines appear.
- **Colour is never the only code.**
  - IgG = near-black Y, with its Fab arms on the membrane.
  - AHG = purple Y with round binding tips.
  - Check cell = red cell with a dark outer ring.
- **Not to scale.** Molecule and cell counts and motion timing are illustrative. They are not measured stoichiometry or kinetics.
- **Face-on red cell.** Drawn with a radial gradient for central pallor. This is schematic morphology.

## Additions or deviations not in the source
1. **[INFERENCE — standard immunology, not literature-verified here]**
   - IgG is bound to the red cell by its Fab arms, with the Fc outward.
   - Anti-IgG binds those Fc ends.
   - Should be verified against a cited immunohematology reference before G6/G8.
2. **Colour change.** The source draws anti-IgG in red. It is purple here, for contrast against red cells.
3. **Morph transitions on frames 2–7** (PowerPoint 2019/365; Fade fallback elsewhere).
   - The XML is present.
   - **Playback has not been tested in PowerPoint**; this environment has none.
4. **Check-cell failure panel.** It is a teaching example. The source does not specify which errors check cells detect.
