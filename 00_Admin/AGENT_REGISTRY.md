# AGENT_REGISTRY.md

15 agents. Least-privilege toolsets. Definitions in `.claude/agents/`; shared rules in `CLAUDE.md`.

| # | Agent | Purpose | Tools | Inputs | Outputs | Depends on | Owns |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **principal-research-director** *(main session)* | Plan, delegate, resolve conflicts by evidence, hold gates, integrate | Read, Grep, Glob + full orchestration | PI instructions; all agent outputs | Master plan, status, decisions, progress reports | — | `00_Admin` |

| 15 | **integrity-auditor** | Independent audit of all domains; PASS / CONDITIONAL PASS / FAIL with blocking authority | Read, Grep, Glob, Bash, Write, PubMed×3, SciSpace | Everything | Audit report, findings by severity, verdict | Independent of all | `10_Audit` |

**Tool-scoping notes.** The auditor has **no Edit** — it cannot modify what it audits. Domain experts have **no Bash/Edit** — they review, they do not alter files. The writer has **no PubMed** — it cannot introduce an unverified citation, by construction. Only the biostatistician and automation agent can execute analysis code.

**Dependency chain:** Methodology → Search → Screening → Extraction → Biostatistics → Writer → Figures → Citation → **Auditor** → Journal, with domain experts consulted throughout and the Director holding every gate.
