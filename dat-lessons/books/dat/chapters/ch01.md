## Errata and decisions

- **IgG orientation**: the antibody is drawn bound to the red cell by its Fab arms, Fc outward, and AHG binds the Fc
  ends. Standard immunology; not stated in the source. All drawings use `dat.js`, which enforces it.
- **Source conflict**: "Delay in the addition of AHG after washing" is listed under both false negative and false
  positive. Narration says "listed under both"; the picture shows it straddling both columns with a ⚑ mark. Do not pick one.
- **Grade criteria (Negative to 4+)** are not described in the source: show the scale as words only, no tube pictures per grade.
- **Wording**: the source writes anti-C3d (principle, mechanisms), anti-C3 (patterns) and C3 (detection limits).
  Keep each as printed where shown.
- **No procedure numbers** (wash solution, spin speed/time) — the source gives none; none are invented.
- Narration simplifications checked against the source: "per cell" removed from detection limits; "suggests" replaced
  by "is seen in" for the classic patterns.

## Shared elements (site/dat/lib/dat.js)

`datCell(p, x, y, r, {check})` red cell group (origin centre; `cell.r0` = radius) · `datIgG(cell, angle, {o})` IgG on a
membrane at angle (0 = up, clockwise) · `datC3d(cell, angle, {o})` · `datAhg(p, r, {x, y, r, o, s})` AHG in its own frame:
binding tips at (±gap/2, 0), body toward +y · `datFcEnd(x, y, r, angle)` world point of an IgG Fc end, so an AHG placed
at the midpoint of two facing Fc ends, rotated along the line between them, bridges them exactly · `datGeo(r).gap` is the
Fc-to-Fc gap that one AHG spans · `datKey(p, items, x, y, t0)` legend. Colours: `DAT.red DAT.igg DAT.c3d DAT.ahg`.

Bridge recipe (used in principle, q1 feedback, checkcells): two cells of radius r with centres D = 2·(r + hinge + fc) + gap
apart along unit u; IgG on each at the membrane angle facing the other; AHG at the midpoint, rotation = atan2(u) in degrees.
`datGeo(r)` gives hinge, fc, gap.
