## 1. Purpose and binding rules

This log exists to make pre-specification checkable rather than assertable.

1. **Before G1 freeze**, the protocol is a draft and changes are recorded as version increments in protocol §19.1, not here.
2. **After G1 freeze, EVERY change to the protocol is logged here — no exception for small, obvious, clarifying or administrative changes.** A change too small to log is a change too small to make.
3. Every entry must record **whether any study result was known at the time of the change**. This field is mandatory and may not be left blank or recorded as "unclear"; if it is genuinely unclear whether results were known, the answer is **YES** and the change is post-hoc.
4. **A change made after any result is known is labelled `POST-HOC` in this log, in the protocol, in the manuscript, and in the abstract.** This is not negotiable and not a matter of presentation (`CLAUDE.md` §1.5).
5. **Outcome switching** — promoting a secondary outcome to primary, or demoting the primary, after results are known — **will be refused** and escalated to the Principal Research Director and thence to Dr. Alanazi. It is not a deviation to be logged; it is a request to be declined.
6. Requests to retrofit methods to results will be refused and escalated.
7. Deviations are reported in the manuscript. A deviation log that exists but is not reported provides no integrity guarantee.

### Changes that are prohibited after freeze except as an explicitly declared POST-HOC deviation (protocol §19.3)

- The primary outcome, its metric, or the seven-class discordance taxonomy (protocol §8.1–§8.3)
- The discordance adjudication procedure, including blinding arrangements (§8.4)
- The escalation trigger list T1–T6 (§7.4)
- The serological reaction-grade cut-off or the phase definitions of D-positive / weak D / serologic D-negative (§7.3)
- The genotype→phenotype mapping table (§8.6)
- The pre-specified subgroup list and sensitivity analyses (§8.5, §12.5)
- Re-adjudication of a locked concordance class (§8.4 step 5)

## 2. Deviation classification

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PLANNED-AMENDMENT** | Change made **before** any study result was known, approved in advance by the PI (and IRB where required). Not post-hoc. |
| **POST-HOC** | Change made **after** any study result was known, for any reason, however defensible. Must be labelled post-hoc in every downstream output. |
| **EXECUTION-DEVIATION** | The protocol was not followed as written in a specific instance (e.g. a sample tested outside the permitted interval). The protocol itself does not change. |
| **ADMINISTRATIVE** | No methodological content (e.g. a corrected typographical error, an updated contact). Still logged; still requires the results-known field. |
